Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. Carma has no account and no server. Carma Pro adds backup and restore, so you can keep a copy somewhere safe yourself.

How do I update my mileage?

Tap the odometer on a vehicle's page, or use Add log on the garage card.

I bought Carma Pro on another phone

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings app, your name, Subscriptions, Carma.

How do I change my ad consent?

In Carma, open Settings and tap Ad privacy choices. The option appears where the law requires it, such as the EU and UK.
