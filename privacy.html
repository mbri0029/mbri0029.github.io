Carma Support Privacy Terms

Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026

What we collect

Carma itself collects nothing. It has no account, no server and no analytics. Your vehicles, documents and photos never leave your phone.

Advertising

The free version shows banner and occasional full-screen ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to select and measure ads. You are asked to consent before any personalised advertising, and declining costs you nothing - the app behaves identically. Carma Pro removes ads entirely, and the ad SDK is never started for a Pro subscriber. Google's use of data is described in its partner sites policy.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. Carma uses RevenueCat to confirm whether you have Carma Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID and your Carma purchase history from Apple - never your name, email or payment details.

Photos and camera

Used only to attach images to your own records. Images are saved to Carma's private storage on your device.

Your rights

Under GDPR you may erase your data at any time, from Settings, without contacting us. If you want a copy of your data and do not have Carma Pro, email us and we will provide it free of charge. You can change your advertising consent at any time from Settings, Ad privacy choices.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, this policy will be updated and you will be asked to opt in before anything is uploaded.
